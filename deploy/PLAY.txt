PARTY KNIGHT - PROTOTYPE (PROTOCOL 3)

Keep PartyKnight.exe and PartyKnight.pck in the same folder.
Connect to your dedicated server's address and UDP port (default 7000).
Choose your name and peasant. You can edit your name again in the lobby.

The host selects games and can shuffle their order. Everyone readies up;
the host starts. Player numbers P1-P4 and their colors remain stable.
Two to four players compete. One player can start unscored practice.
The dedicated server is required even for practice; this client does not host.

BALLISTA RUN
Runners: WASD moves; left click shoves in your facing direction.
Reach the gold finish line using cover. No jumping.
Shooter: mouse aims; left click fires. Four shots, then a 3-second reload.
Everyone shoots once. EACH HEAT LASTS UP TO 60 SECONDS, ending early when
all runners have finished or been eliminated.

LANCE
WASD moves; left click shoves. Find an OPEN stall before the sharks charge.
Four-second warnings show closing stalls. Fewer stalls remain each wave.
Last survivor wins. Three rounds, at most 45 seconds each.

COIN COLLECTION
Tap E to collect. Stop tapping to retract in 0.25 seconds.
The blade shakes for 1-4 seconds before falling. If it catches your hand,
you stop collecting for the round, but KEEP your coins.
Three rounds, at most 35 seconds or six blade cycles each.

Each completed game awards 3/2/1/0 match points after combining its rounds.
Ties share points and skip occupied ranks. Highest match total wins.
You respawn next round or heat. TAB changes runner spectator targets.
ESC opens settings. Settings control fullscreen, volume, and blood.
Solo Ballista is an unopposed 60-second course; solo play awards no points.
